## The director's notes (point 14)
*What was wrong:* JUST / SOME / FRIES slammed onto the page like a comic title was loud and decorative; the gull ended the game by being told what it had wanted. Nothing of what the player had actually lived was in it, and the big brother was a prop.
*What I decided:* the ending belongs to the plain fry that fell on the floor in the opening - and to everything the gull did in between.
1. **The bite.** The gull steps out of the corner onto open floor, the camera is as low as the opening's close-up, time slows. "...oh." Salt. Warm. "...that's it?" ... "...that's it."
2. **The memories.** While it chews, small tilted polaroids drift in, one at a time, with one line each, in the order the player did things - **only things that really happened**: the first fry, a coffee, a cocktail, ice cream, STARLIGHT, the first fish and the biggest one (by its real name and length), a cloud, the sun, a falling star, a friend, a gift, a rainbow fry, a wall... (`GS.memories`, filled by `GS.award` and a few events; saved with the run). The last polaroid is always **a fry on the floor**: "i heard it fall, that first morning. i didn't know it was for me." Then: "everything was warm. the sun, the star... this was warm too."
3. **The big brother comes down from his roof** (he has talked with the player three times on the roof by now: after 3, 10 and 18 fries, only if the player sits next to him). He says what the player already knows: *I've been up on that roof, thinking about it. Chain. Shades. Hat. Coat. I got everything. So why am I still hungry?* The old man - who has sat there all along - drops one more fry, with the same little "tk." as in the opening. "...What do YOU want?" And the little gull says its first words in the whole game: **"...just some fries."** The big brother takes his shades off and eats. "...same." One more fry falls ("...keep them coming."). The picture pulls back: two gulls, an old man, a plain morning.
4. The old cards come back, answered: *THIS WAS AN AGE OF MAGIC ... AND ON A PIER, TWO SEAGULLS SHARED ONE PLAIN FRY. - IT WAS ENOUGH.* Then the cover as a poster and the credits.
Nothing is explained. The title of the game is a sentence somebody finally says.
