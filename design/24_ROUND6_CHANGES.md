## Decisions that were not specified
* Gold press = 2 points (ends a short rhythm early); a perfect = no miss and at least 70 % golds.
* Coffee / cocktail also burn away when the gull is hit; ice cream only on landing (hits are shrugged off).
* A rainbow fry is one circle with two waves; cloud / sun are one wave with the narrowest window.
* The dead escape-check code was removed (`--flee` now just grabs the tutorial fries).
