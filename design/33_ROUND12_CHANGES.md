The distant gull no longer calls during the ending or under a film moment, and is 2 dB quieter. In a calm town (watch < 0.7) resting gulls are visited by children with a rainbow fry **much more often** (three of six events instead of two of five, a 35 s wait instead of 90 s; the kind children on the street wait 60 s instead of 150 s). |
