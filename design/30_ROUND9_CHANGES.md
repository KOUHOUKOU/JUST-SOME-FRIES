# 30 · Round 9 — "the last quality upgrade" (2026-10-08: 10 points of the director's notes)

The plan that was approved is `docs/29_ROUND9_PLAN.md`. This file says what was built and how it is tested. Numbers live in `TUNING_NOTES.md` ("Round 9").
New dev modes: `--r9speed` (can the best gull reach the sky's needs?), `--r9cine` (the real cinematic moments, a screenshot per second, gull share of the picture measured),
`--r9quiet` (the light film mode), `--r9tab` (Gull Sight pillars), `--r9trail` (star trail), `--r9sky` (weather + rainbow + the 14-judgement catch), `--shotcheck` (the opening),
and `--r8e` now measures the ending's camera too.

| # | Note | What the game does now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title and ending music: adapt a mature piece | Both are now **adaptations of public-domain classics**, played by real sampled instruments (FluidSynth + GeneralUser GS; `tools/audio/music9.py`, `music9_end.py`, `sf_render.py`). **Title "Morning at the Pier"** = Grieg, *Morning Mood* (G major, 6/8): nylon guitar arpeggios, flute, clarinet, harp, strings, horn, celesta; the tune goes through its pentatonic question, a minor third higher for the sunrise, and the last bars stop on the dominant: it never answers (63 s loop). **Ending** is in two pieces: *ending_mem* (while the polaroids drift: the same question as a quiet solo piano in D-flat, joined voice by voice, 48 s loop) and *ending* = Dvorak's **Largo** (the melody notes are the real ones, read from a reference MIDI of the score; English horn, clarinet, oboe, strings, solo violin on the climb, harp). They cross-fade (3 s) when the big brother lands; the climb reaches its high F at about "...just some fries." and the last long D-flat (home) rings under "...same."; the coda asks the *Morning Mood* question once more in the celesta, and this time it ends on the 1. Credits name the sources. |
| 2 | The camera (picture 1) | `ui/cine_cam.gd`, a camera director: shots are asked for by **size and background**, not metres (formula: `dist = width / (fraction * 2 tan(fov/2) * 1.78)`). It searches the circle around the subject for an angle where the line is free (physics rays **and** people, dogs and fries, which are not solid), prefers a backdrop with depth (not a wall, and not a pale gull on pale water), puts the subject on a third with lead room, and only pushes in slowly. The old `_orbit` (1.3-2.4 m from a 3 m-wingspan gull) is gone. The opening's hungry-gull panel and the old man's hand, every ending shot and the big brother's roof talks use it; the ending clears the café's people near the camera. Tests: `--r9cine`, `--shotcheck`, `--r8e` fail when a gull fills more than ~60 % of the width or the lens is within 2.4 m. |
| 3 | TAB: clearer fries | In Gull Sight every fry emblem is 2x bigger (the core colour is still the stat), and every silver / gold / diamond / rainbow fry sends a **pillar of light to the sky**: silver (white-blue, 70 m), gold (130 m), diamond (ice-blue with a climbing spark, 220 m), rainbow (cycling colours, two sparks, 350 m). A legend under "GULL SIGHT" shows the four colours. The plain fry never has one. |
| 4 | Sky difficulty | Needs (gauge = 6 x m/s): **cloud 255, shooting star 262, rainbow 266, sun 272**. Ceiling: gold SONIC + three drinks + STARLIGHT = 277 (silver SONIC 247, diamond 298; `--r9speed` flies it). So a silver gull can catch nothing in the sky, a gold gull has 22 / 15 / 11 / 5 points of slack, and the sun is the hardest thing in the game. All four are **5 circles x 2 judgements** (the rainbow 7 x 2), the same bands as a diamond fry (x1.5 in STARLIGHT, which they always need). The speedometer scale grows to 310 when the top speed passes 155. A real star is only sent to a gull that could catch it. |
| 5 | Rainbow fry | 5 circles x 2 judgements (10). |
| 6 | Everything with a circle is judged twice | Hats, clothes, drinks, ice cream, the ball: 1 x 2. Fish: common 1 x 2, rare 3 x 2, legendary 5 x 2. |
| 7 | Star trail | The caught star is a small golden star behind the gull and leaves **golden four-pointed stars that stay in the air and fade over ~3 s** (more of them the faster the gull flies; non-additive, never white; a star that floats past the lens fades). |
| 8 | Cloud kinds | White (15), grey (6) and rain clouds (5, heavy and flat). A rain cloud rains **only while open sea is under it** (five sample points under its footprint) and stops over land. |
| 9 | Rainbow | A rain cloud and a white cloud drift together over the southern sea with a seven-band rainbow between them (it appears while it rains, `world/rainbow_pair.gd`). Quest **CATCH A RAINBOW** (17 fries): the catch point is the place on the arch nearest to the gull; **7 circles x 2 = 14 judgements**, each circle one colour of the rainbow with its golden ring; reward: the 19th accessory (a little rainbow over the head), a memory polaroid, an award, a short film. The accessory page is now three rows of seven. |
| 10 | Film mode | **Light** (`hud.quiet_moment`, the gull keeps flying, nothing is locked or slowed, thin bars, 1-4 lines in the middle of the picture on a soft dark band): first coffee, first cocktail, first ice cream, the first fish (its real name, length and weight), a cloud, the fish book. **Real** (`story_scenes.gd`, 10-20 s, bars, slow time, 3-5 shots, its own music): the first STARLIGHT (17 s), a shooting star (16 s), **the sun (12 s, the same three words as before, now with a film around them)**, the rainbow (13 s), all 24 fries (19 s). A 4 s breather between two moments; each plays once. |
