# 02 — PROGRESSION, ENDING, AND ALL PLAYER-FACING COPY

This file is authoritative for English copy. Do not improvise new philosophical text.

## Opening

Title/menu:

`JUST SOME FRIES`

First objective:

`JUST THREE FRIES.`

Counter:

`0 / 3`

## Tutorial progression

On first tutorial success:

`GULL SENSE 1 / 3`

On second:

`GULL SENSE 2 / 3`

On third:

`GULL SENSE 3 / 3`

Then:

`GULL SENSE COMPLETE`

After reveal:

`3 / 7`

## Achievement copy

Achievements are hidden; no checklist UI.

- `EARLY BIRD` — Eat the Ordinary Fry before Gull Sense reaches 3/3.
- `CLEAN GETAWAY` — Take the child's fry without entering ALERT or being hit.
- `MAGPIE` — Bring any non-food object to the nest.
- `RUNNING ON FUMES` — Return to the nest with ≤10 stamina.
- `SOMEWHERE ELSE` — Cross the far-sea wind boundary.
- `ENOUGH` — Eat the Ordinary Fry after reaching 7/7.

If achievements are cut, do not replace them with another progression system.
