## Decisions that were not specified
* Fry colours are rainbow hues; yellow is only the starters. No fry adds cruise speed.
* Gold fries hang at low, "table-like" places (shelves, tables, roofs, boat deck, lifeguard tower...), diamond fries at the sky anchors (ferris wheel, crane hooks, turbine...).
* A gull held by a guided approach cannot crash while the rings run (`gull_player._fly` returns early during the lock and the close-up).
* The hill lost a quarter of its identical houses (pocket gardens instead); 40 % of the remaining houses have flat roofs (laundry).
