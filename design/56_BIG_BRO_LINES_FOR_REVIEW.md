# 大哥海鸥全部台词（第 20 轮审阅稿）

每次拜访只触发**一段**。点开“E 和大哥聊聊”或飞到他旁边时，先放玩家**还没听过的关键对白**（按下表顺序），没有就放一段**闲聊**（按进度分四档，不连续重复）。

## 关键对白（每个存档只出现一次）

### ① 初见（3 根薯条）  `chat1`
- **大哥**：看见你落地了。干净利落。不赖嘛，小家伙。  
  <sub>Saw you land. Clean. Not bad, kid.</sub>
- **大哥**：快胜过壮，壮胜过吵。  
  <sub>Fast beats strong. Strong beats loud.</sub>
- **大哥**：去让天空紧张紧张吧。我就在这儿，看起来很贵。  
  <sub>Go make the sky nervous. I'll be here. Looking expensive.</sub>

### ② 十根薯条  `chat2`
- **大哥**：十根了。你飞得像赶着去干什么似的。  
  <sub>Ten fries. You fly like you're late for something.</sub>
- **大哥**：我以前也那么快。后来我发现了配饰。  
  <sub>I was fast like that once. Then I found accessories.</sub>
- **大哥**：帽子很沉，想要的东西也是。  
  <sub>...Hats are heavy. So is wanting things.</sub>

### ③ 十八根（越来越像他）  `chat3`
- **大哥**：十八根。你觉得我们要往哪儿去，小家伙？  
  <sub>Eighteen. Where do you think we're headed, kid?</sub>
- **大哥**：十九根吧，我猜。那可不是方向，那是算术。  
  <sub>...Nineteen, I suppose. That's not a direction. That's arithmetic.</sub>
- **大哥**：什么都有，说起来挺好笑：它占地方，就占在这里面。  
  <sub>Funny thing about having everything: it takes up room. In here.</sub>
- **大哥**：你也会眯眼了。……你越来越像我了。  
  <sub>You've got the squint now. ...You're starting to look like me.</sub>
- **大哥**：不知道这算不算夸奖。对你，对我，都一样。  
  <sub>Not sure that's a compliment. To either of us.</sub>
- **大哥**：还饿吗？嗯，我也是。  
  <sub>Still hungry? ...Yeah. Me too.</sub>

### ④ 全部 24 根（第一根薯条）  `chat4`
- **大哥**：全部二十四根。整条光谱。  
  <sub>All twenty-four. The whole spectrum.</sub>
- **大哥**：我也曾经全都有过。闭着眼睛都叫得出每一种的名字。  
  <sub>I had them all, once. I could name every one in my sleep.</sub>
- **大哥**：那些年一直在追魔法薯条。快的，亮的，会唱歌的。  
  <sub>Years of chasing magic fries. The fast ones. The shiny ones. The ones that sing.</sub>
- **大哥**：追着追着，我忘了第一根薯条在哪儿，是什么味道。  
  <sub>And somewhere in all that, I lost track of the first one. Where it was. What it tasted like.</sub>
- **你**：还饿吗？  
  <sub>...still hungry?</sub>
- **大哥**：一直饿。这就是它的把戏。  
  <sub>Always. That's the trick of it.</sub>
- **大哥**：它从来就不是盘子里看得见的那种东西。  
  <sub>It never was the kind you can see on a plate.</sub>

### 戴上第一件配饰  `bro_hat`
- **大哥**：这帽子。……你在哪儿找到的？  
  <sub>That hat. ...Where'd you find it?</sub>
- **大哥**：不，别告诉我。帽子的故事，该由帽子自己留着。  
  <sub>No. Don't tell me. A hat should keep its own story.</sub>
- **大哥**：压低点戴。这上面的太阳没礼貌。  
  <sub>Wear it low. The sun is rude up here.</sub>

### 喝过咖啡/鸡尾酒/冰淇淋  `bro_drinks`
- **大哥**：你身上一股咖啡馆、酒吧加冷柜的味儿。  
  <sub>You smell like a cafe, a bar and a freezer.</sub>
- **大哥**：……佩服。  
  <sub>...Respect.</sub>
- **大哥**：我从不信任那种没法一边端着鸡尾酒、一边揣着怨气的海鸥。  
  <sub>I never trusted a gull who couldn't hold a cocktail and a grudge at the same time.</sub>

### 第一次抓到鱼  `bro_fish`
- **大哥**：我在你身上闻到了海的味道。  
  <sub>I smelled the sea on you.</sub>
- **大哥**：这么说，它给了你点东西。它从没给过我什么。  
  <sub>So it gave you something. It never gave me anything.</sub>
- **大哥**：……我从没礼貌地问过。  
  <sub>...I never asked politely.</sub>
- **大哥**：走的时候记得说声谢谢，小家伙。水是记礼数的。  
  <sub>Say thank you on your way out, kid. The water remembers manners.</sub>

### 彩虹时间  `bro_starlight`
- **大哥**：前两天划过天空的那道光。什么颜色都有。吵得很。  
  <sub>That streak across the sky the other day. Every colour. Very loud.</sub>
- **大哥**：我以前也留下过一道。  
  <sub>I used to leave one of those.</sub>
- **大哥**：码头上的人还以为是架飞机。  
  <sub>People on the pier thought it was a plane.</sub>
- **大哥**：……是我。差不多吧。  
  <sub>...It was me. Mostly.</sub>

### 有海鸥/孩子/陌生人对你示好  `bro_friend`
- **大哥**：我看见有只海鸥落在你旁边，就为了打声招呼。  
  <sub>I saw a gull land next to you. Just to say hello.</sub>
- **大哥**：现在流行这样了？  
  <sub>Do they do that now?</sub>
- **大哥**：我那会儿，大家互相不理睬。出于尊重，还有骄傲。  
  <sub>In my day we ignored each other. Out of respect. And pride.</sub>
- **大哥**：也怕那顶帽子。  
  <sub>And fear of the hat.</sub>

### 叼到云  `bro_sky_cloud`
- **大哥**：你戴过一朵云。……感觉怎么样？  
  <sub>You wore a cloud. ...How was it?</sub>
- **你**：软软的。  
  <sub>...soft.</sub>
- **大哥**：软软的。对。我忘了天空是这样的。  
  <sub>Soft. Yes. That's what I'd forgotten about the sky.</sub>
- **大哥**：人人抬头只看见高度，没人提起那些软垫子。  
  <sub>Everybody looks up and sees height. Nobody mentions the cushions.</sub>

### 叼到雨云  `bro_sky_raincloud`
- **大哥**：一朵雨云。你专属的天气。  
  <sub>A rain cloud. Your own private weather.</sub>
- **大哥**：我多年前也有过一朵。它只往我的帽子上下雨。  
  <sub>I had one of those, years ago. It only ever rained on my hat.</sub>
- **大哥**：……所以这顶帽子防水。大体上。  
  <sub>...That's why the hat is waterproof. Mostly.</sub>

### 叼到流星  `bro_sky_star`
- **大哥**：一颗流星。居然叼在你嘴里。  
  <sub>A falling star. In your beak, no less.</sub>
- **大哥**：许愿了吗？  
  <sub>Did you wish?</sub>
- **你**：嘴里塞满了。  
  <sub>...beak was full.</sub>
- **大哥**：哈。好。许愿是留给嘴里空着的海鸥的。  
  <sub>Ha. Good. Wishes are for gulls with nothing in their beaks.</sub>
- **大哥**：我许过一次愿。要一顶帽子。……看看后来怎么样了。  
  <sub>I wished once. For a hat. ...Look how that went.</sub>

### 叼到月亮  `bro_sky_moon`
- **大哥**：你抓住了月亮。我试过一次，它笑了。  
  <sub>You caught the moon. I tried once. It laughed.</sub>
- **大哥**：我冲着它飞了一整夜。  
  <sub>I flew at it all night.</sub>
- **大哥**：天亮时我到了岛的另一头，它还在上面，平静得像个盘子。  
  <sub>By morning I was at the far end of the island and it was still up there. Calm as a plate.</sub>
- **大哥**：……结果它跑去坐在了你嘴里。真有它的。  
  <sub>...Then it went and sat in your beak. Typical.</sub>

### 叼到太阳  `bro_sky_sun`
- **大哥**：太阳，叼在嘴里。  
  <sub>The sun. In a beak.</sub>
- **大哥**：暖和吗？  
  <sub>Warm?</sub>
- **你**：暖暖的。  
  <sub>...warm.</sub>
- **大哥**：我有一回叼了它一秒钟。留了个印子。  
  <sub>I held it for one second, once. It left a mark.</sub>
- **大哥**：……你以为这墨镜只是摆设？  
  <sub>...You think these shades are for show?</sub>

### 穿过彩虹  `bro_sky_rainbow`
- **大哥**：一头穿过彩虹。所有的颜色都有，却没一种有味道。  
  <sub>Straight through a rainbow. Every colour there is, and none of them has a taste.</sub>
- **大哥**：怪了。你本以为会有的。  
  <sub>Strange. You'd think they would.</sub>
- **你**：有彩虹的味道。  
  <sub>...it tastes like a rainbow.</sub>
- **大哥**：……有道理。  
  <sub>...Fair.</sub>

### 五样天空都叼过之后  `chat_sky`
- **大哥**：云，星，太阳，月亮，彩虹。整片天空。  
  <sub>Cloud, star, sun, moon, rainbow. The whole sky.</sub>
- **大哥**：没人能得到整片天空。它不是那种东西。  
  <sub>Nobody gets the whole sky. It's not that kind of thing.</sub>
- **大哥**：而你回到的，是一片屋顶。  
  <sub>And you came back down to a roof.</sub>
- **大哥**：……有意思。  
  <sub>...Interesting.</sub>

### 通关后第一次回到屋顶  `free_intro`
- **大哥**：你回来了。薯条没了，你倒是回来了。  
  <sub>...You're back. The fry is gone, and you're back.</sub>
- **大哥**：很好。天空一点没变小。  
  <sub>Good. The sky didn't get any smaller.</sub>
- **大哥**：想找个伴就按 E。  
  <sub>Press E when you want company.</sub>

  <sub>I'll be here. Without the chain. ...Still looking expensive.</sub>

## 闲聊（没有新的关键对白时，每次按 E 一段）

### 闲聊 · 0–9 根（话少，冷）

- **大哥**：今天风不错。  
  <sub>Wind's good today.</sub>
- **大哥**：别浪费了。  
  <sub>Don't waste it.</sub>

- **大哥**：留神老爷子的桌子。  
  <sub>Mind the old man's table.</sub>
- **大哥**：倒不是说他会发现。……还是留神点。  
  <sub>Not that he'd notice. ...Still.</sub>

- **大哥**：从小的开始，一点点往上。  
  <sub>Start small. Work up.</sub>
- **大哥**：谁都这样。我也一样。  
  <sub>Everybody does. Even me.</sub>

- **大哥**：从上面看，大海是平的。  
  <sub>The sea looks flat from up here.</sub>
- **大哥**：……其实不是。  
  <sub>...It isn't.</sub>

- **大哥**：抬头，小家伙。翅膀张开。  
  <sub>Chin up, kid. Wings out.</sub>
- **大哥**：剩下的都是细节。  
  <sub>The rest is details.</sub>

### 闲聊 · 10–17 根（冷幽默，教两句）

- **大哥**：你一紧张，圈就缩。  
  <sub>The rings shrink when you're nervous.</sub>
- **大哥**：这不是规律。我只是注意到了。  
  <sub>That's not a rule. I just noticed.</sub>

- **大哥**：歇脚，屋顶比树强。  
  <sub>Roofs are better for resting than trees.</sub>
- **大哥**：屋顶稳得住。树爱嚼舌根。  
  <sub>Roofs hold still. Trees gossip.</sub>

- **大哥**：有没有注意到，人群盯的是你的脚，不是你的脸？  
  <sub>Notice how a crowd watches your feet, not your face?</sub>
- **大哥**：海鸥和小偷，规矩一样：膝盖以下要看起来无辜。  
  <sub>Gulls and thieves, same rule: look innocent from the knees down.</sub>

- **大哥**：我曾有条规矩：嘴里塞满、脑袋空空，不许起飞。  
  <sub>I had a rule once: never fly with a full beak and an empty head.</sub>
- **大哥**：……天天违反。  
  <sub>...Broke it daily.</sub>

### 闲聊 · 18 根以上（开始交心）

- **大哥**：傍晚，整个码头都会变成薯条的颜色。  
  <sub>Late in the afternoon the whole pier goes the colour of a fry.</sub>
- **大哥**：……我没说过这话。忘了吧。  
  <sub>...I didn't say that. Forget it.</sub>

- **大哥**：我认识一只海鸥，只吃掉下来的东西，从不抢。  
  <sub>I knew a gull who only ever ate what fell. Never took a thing.</sub>
- **大哥**：我当时觉得他是个傻子。  
  <sub>I thought he was a fool.</sub>
- **大哥**：……可他看上去总是吃饱了的样子。  
  <sub>...He always looked fed.</sub>

- **大哥**：我所有的东西，都穿在身上。  
  <sub>Everything I own, I wear.</sub>
- **大哥**：怪了，沉的从来不是外面这层。  
  <sub>Funny. It's never the outside that feels heavy.</sub>

- **大哥**：我留着一张清单，写着我拥有的一切。  
  <sub>I keep a list of everything I have.</sub>
- **大哥**：很长。靠近底下有一处空着，原先该有样东西。  
  <sub>It's very long. There's a gap near the bottom where something used to be.</sub>

### 闲聊 · 通关之后（温暖，带回忆；另有几条实用提示）

- **大哥**：那位老爷子，还在掉薯条吗？  
  <sub>That old man. Still drops them?</sub>
- **你**：每天早上。  
  <sub>...every morning.</sub>
- **大哥**：那就好。  
  <sub>Good.</sub>

- **大哥**：盐味。现在我记得最清楚的就是它。  
  <sub>Salt. That's what I remember best now.</sub>
- **大哥**：盐味，还有一声轻轻的响。嗒。  
  <sub>Salt, and a small sound. tk.</sub>

- **大哥**：怪了，帽子轻了。  
  <sub>Funny. The hat is lighter.</sub>
- **大哥**：还是那顶帽子。  
  <sub>Same hat.</sub>

- **大哥**：把眯眼正经教教我。  
  <sub>Show me the squint properly.</sub>
- **你**：这样。  
  <sub>...like this.</sub>
- **大哥**：这样？  
  <sub>Like this?</sub>
- **你**：不对。  
  <sub>...no.</sub>
- **大哥**：……我再练练。  
  <sub>...I'll practise.</sub>

- **大哥**：它送到这儿的时候，还是热的。  
  <sub>It was still warm when it got up here.</sub>
- **大哥**：……我常想起这事。  
  <sub>...I think about that.</sub>

- **大哥**：坐。风景还是那个风景。  
  <sub>Sit. The view's the same.</sub>
- **大哥**：陪伴可不一样了。  
  <sub>The company isn't.</sub>

## 结尾（屋顶一场，重写）

- **大哥**：……帽子可真不少，小家伙。  
  <sub>...That's a lot of hats, kid.</sub>
- **大哥**：我懂的。  
  <sub>I'd know.</sub>

*— 小海鸥把薯条放在两只之间 —*

- **大哥**：……那是什么。  
  <sub>...What's that.</sub>
- **你**：不就是一根薯条嘛。  
  <sub>...it's just a fry.</sub>
- **大哥**：它不发光。  
  <sub>It doesn't glow.</sub>
- **大哥**：它不会让你变快。  
  <sub>It doesn't make you fast.</sub>
- **大哥**：……它什么用都没有。  
  <sub>...It doesn't do anything.</sub>
- **你**：不过是些薯条。  
  <sub>...just some fries.</sub>

*— 小海鸥身上所有的装饰落下 —*

- **大哥**：我也是。  
  <sub>...same.</sub>
- **大哥**：我这一身，花了好些年才穿上。  
  <sub>Mine took years to put on.</sub>
- **大哥**：反过来脱，我从没试过。  
  <sub>I never tried the other direction.</sub>

*— 他摘下墨镜：第一次露出眼睛 —*

- **大哥**：……是我记得的那个颜色。  
  <sub>...It's the colour I remembered.</sub>
- **大哥**：你一路把它叼到了这上面。  
  <sub>You carried it all the way up here.</sub>
- **大哥**：……还是热的。  
  <sub>...It's still warm.</sub>

*— 他慢慢吃掉它 —*

- **大哥**：……啊。  
  <sub>...Oh.</sub>
- **大哥**：盐味。  
  <sub>Salt.</sub>
- **大哥**：我都忘了，它这么小。  
  <sub>I'd forgotten it was this small.</sub>
- **你**：嗯。  
  <sub>...yeah.</sub>

*— 两只转向大海，海浪声轻轻垫在下面 —*

- *旁白*：他把金链子摘了下来。没人要他这么做。  
  <sub>he took off the chain. nobody asked him to.</sub>
- **大哥**：帽子我留着。  
  <sub>I'm keeping the hat.</sub>
- **你**：好帽子。  
  <sub>...good hat.</sub>
- **大哥**：……确实是顶好帽子。  
  <sub>...It is a good hat.</sub>
- **大哥**：那位老爷子，现在还会掉吗？  
  <sub>That old man. Does he still drop them?</sub>
- **你**：每天早上。  
  <sub>...every morning.</sub>

*— 远处楼下：嗒。（薯条落地的小声响）—*

- **大哥**：……那就好。  
  <sub>...Good.</sub>
- *旁白*：没有什么闪亮的，也没有什么快的。  
  <sub>nothing shiny. nothing fast.</sub>
- *旁白*：只有一根薯条，和一个一起吃的人。  
  <sub>just a fry, and somebody to eat it with.</sub>
