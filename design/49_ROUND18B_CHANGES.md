## The seven points
1. **The circles are smaller.** `ring` EASY 1.35 -> **0.68**, HARD 1.1 -> **0.62** (classic 1.0). The white waves, the dark scrim and the glow are as before: the logic of the white circle was good.
2. **No slow first circle.** `first_slow` = 1.0 in both modes: the first wave is as fast as the others, its bands are the same width as the next ones. The count-in ticks stay (EASY 2, HARD 1: the soft ticks are the "1, 2" before the first wave), `first_lead` is shorter (0.3 / 0.1).
3. **A brighter click.** Every note of a tune now has a **'ting' of tinkle bells an octave higher** on top (new instruments `tinkle` (GM 112) and `xylo` (GM 13) in the note bank, bank extended to MIDI 100), the plucked voices have a high shelf (+4..6 dB above 2.2 kHz, `BRIGHT` in `r18_audio.py`), the small fries (starter / silver / fish) **sing an octave higher** (`Tunes.LIFT`). A gold press (two points) is now `Tunes.gold`: two notes of the tune 70 ms apart + a glockenspiel sparkle + **a little upward run of three bells on the pentatonic scale**: the "yes!" of the game.
4. **The welcome of a friend gull is a song.** `friend_welcome.wav` (`r18_audio.py friend_welcome`, 126 bpm, C major, I - IV - V - I, about 4.5 s + tail): a harp run as it lands, a music-box tune that skips up to a high held note, plucked strings bouncing on the off-beats, a harp bed, a warm pad, an "aah" choir that swells under the last chord and a run of tinkling bells. It plays when the friend lands (`rest_events.gd`), the first gull of a called flock brings the same song, the others a bell each. The hearts that rise ring a bell each, **climbing up the pentatonic scale** (`heart_fx(k)`), a soft pop under it. A short "hello" plays when the friend is seen coming.
5. **The families speed up (EASY and HARD).** `ladder` (the time of one wave and of the gap): EASY starter 1.0 / silver 0.92 / gold 0.84 / diamond 0.76; HARD 1.0 / 0.94 / 0.88 / 0.82 (on top of HARD's 0.88). `band_lad` (EASY only): the fat bands get thinner with the family: 1.0 / 0.9 / 0.8 / 0.72. The music of a diamond fry is quicker (and more connected) than the music of a starter fry; the fry is no longer easy at the top.
6. **OCEAN TIME fish.** On the water with OCEAN TIME running: **hold TAB** = the fish nearest to the middle of the view is locked (a turning ring in the colour of its rarity, its name, the speed it needs, "SPACE - LAUNCH AT IT"). The lock stays when TAB is let go, until the fish is gone. **SPACE then launches straight at the place the fish WILL be** (`snatch_controller.fish_aim_point`, the leap is a known arc: `fish_spot.predict`), and for the first second of the climb the beak is drawn towards it. A fish caught in a dive or a flat flight is judged as ever. More fish: the leaps happen where the gull is (16-58 m, half of them in the half of the sea it looks at), more at the same time (9), the pause between two leaps is 0.25-1.1 s, the warning is 1.5 s, and **the sea always has a fish of every rarity within 85 m of the gull** (a small director in `fish_spot.gd`: a missing rarity is planned first; the test samples it: 100 %). Time in the air +30 % (3.5 / 4.0 / 4.7 s).
7. **HARD is a rhythm game.** (`GS.dp("single")`.) ONE band, the gold one, as wide as the old green band (the old gold band is gone). **The tune plays by itself**: every wave is a beat of it, quietly, in the middle of its band (`Tunes.beat`); an E counts only when the white wave is in the band; **a hit adds a bright voice on top of its beat** (`Tunes.hit`: xylophone + tinkle + the voice of the family, shinier with the streak) and is worth one point; a quarter of the beats may be missed (`need = n - n/4`), a missed beat is just a beat that does not shine, and the melody goes on. The circles show only the gold ring; the coach, the hints and the controls page say "ON THE BEAT / ON THE GOLD" in HARD.
8. **The goals board folds with Q.** A small keycap `Q  HIDE` sits in the corner of the board; folded, a small tab `Q  GOALS` stays where the board was, so that the player remembers it is there. The choice is kept in `user://settings.cfg`. **The quick U-turn moved from Q to R** (the coach, the hints and the controls page say R).
