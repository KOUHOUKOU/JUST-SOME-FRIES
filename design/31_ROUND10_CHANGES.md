# 31 · Round 10 — "shorter, funnier, quieter" (2026-10-08: 9 points of the director's notes)

New dev modes: `--r10beats` (every beat and every film moment, timed and filmed, `--only=drink_coffee,fish,...` for the beats only), `--r10npc` (the people never enter a wall:
spawn audit + thousands of steps of the real movement code), `--r10misc` (the big brother's line, the sky's needs, the fish, the quiet way to the plain fry).
`--r9speed`, `--r9cine`, `--r9quiet` were adapted to the new numbers.

| # | Note | What the game does now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Moments are too long: drinks, fish... must be 5-10 s | The light moments are now **BEATS** (`story.gd beat`, `story_scenes.gd beat_moment`): the gull keeps flying, nothing is locked or slowed, the interface fades. A splash of the thing's colour, **one big hand-lettered comic word on an ink-edged starburst** (`BZZZT!` coffee, `GLUG~` cocktail, `BRRR!` ice cream, `SPLASH!` / `OOH!` / `WHOA!!` fish by rarity, `FLUFF!` cloud, `GULP.` fish book), then one or two lines of the gull's thoughts in a yellow caption box, typed fast. **4-8 s** (coffee 6.2, cocktail 5.9, ice cream 6.5, fish 6, cloud 7.5, fish book 7.8 in the bot's measurement). Beats queue behind each other, never over a film moment. |
| 2 | Cloud / sun / rainbow: ~10 s, funnier, memorable | The film moments were cut to **6.6-8.1 s**: bars in 0.5 s, 2-3 camera shots, 2-3 lines typed at 46 characters a second, each with a word slammed on the picture: `STARLIGHT` (a triple hit `BZZZT! GLUG~ BRRR!` first, then `WHEEEEEE!`), `ZING!` + a star that decides to stay (the shooting star), **`FWOOM!`** (the sun, the same three sentences as before), `TA-DAA!` (the rainbow), `24 / 24` (all the fries; the camera rises over the town and ends on "so why does it still... rumble?"). The cloud and the fish book are beats (point 1). |
| 3 | The last fry: too forced; the ending too deliberate | **The way to the plain fry is quiet now.** Only two whispers by the clock (3 and 7 minutes of hunger) and one by the count (18 fries), none pointing anywhere; the old man no longer waves; "nothing ordinary glows." is gone from Gull Sight; the halo is a faint glow only within 7-9 m of the fry (no chime); the steam is a thread, visible only within ~25 m. **The ending** is about half as long and flows: the bite ("...oh." "salt." "warm."), then at most **five** memory polaroids that drift by on their own (no commentary; the music carries them), "it fell right at my feet.", the big brother lands, three lines (not four), the old man drops one fry, "...What do YOU want?" "...just some fries." He eats: "...same." No second drop, no "keep them coming": a slow pull-back and out (about 62 s from the bite, was ~110 s). |
| 4 | The big brother's line only when near | His bubble exists **only while he is talking** (it used to leave its black outline behind) and is dropped if the gull is further than 14 m; it is drawn above the sea / horizon haze (render priority 120). He still says one line when the gull has been sitting near him for a while. |
| 5 | People stuck in walls | `npc/nav.gd`: every walker, runner and dog moves through `Nav.step` - two balls (waist and knee) are swept a step ahead against buildings and props (the ground is not an obstacle: people follow the hills); a blocked person slides along the wall or waits and then skips to the next waypoint; kids who run at the gull leave breadcrumbs and walk home along them (a kid who is blocked gives up its run); a dog's lunge stops at a wall. A person who was placed inside a prop is lifted out to the nearest free spot (<= 2.4 m) the first time they are near. Test `--r10npc`: 161 people, 0 inside a wall; 19 strollers x 1500 steps, 5 patrolling owners x 1200 steps, 11 kids x 12 directions: 0 entries into a wall; every kid gets home. |
| 6 | The sky is too hard; fish can be harder | Gauge needs (6 x m/s): **cloud 225, shooting star 232, rainbow 238, sun 244** - a silver-SONIC gull (20 m/s) with all three drinks and STARLIGHT reaches 247. **Fish: rare 120 -> 132, legendary 144 -> 186** (gold SONIC plus a drink or two; legendary stays 5 circles x 2 judgements). |
| 7 | The tutorial should teach "gull time" | The slow time of a lock-on is named: the first fry's instruction reads "THE WORLD SLOWS DOWN: THIS IS GULL TIME. PRESS E WHEN A WHITE RING HITS THE GREEN", the second "GULL TIME AGAIN...", and after the first two fries the gull works it out in small print ("that slow moment... that's a gull's whole trick." / "the closer the fry, the slower the world. it's called gull time."). During every lock-on the words **G U L L   T I M E** sit at the top edge (bright for a new gull, a whisper for an old hand). |
| 8 | More comic character in the animations | See 1 and 2: every moment has its own lettering, colour and sound; the captions are the narrator's yellow boxes of the opening / ending. The beats' captions sit lower left, big; the film moments' thoughts under the bars are bigger (29 px). |
| 9 | Music | **Real orchestras.** Everything important is now a public-domain work in a public-domain recording (Musopen Symphony etc., via Wikimedia Commons; the downloads were approved by the director): title = Grieg, *Morning Mood* (the whole 3:49 as one seamless loop); memories = Chopin, Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2; ending = Dvorak, Largo (the long build to its great climax - it lands on "...just some fries." - then the theme comes home quietly, 150 s); STARLIGHT film = the opening of Mendelssohn's *Italian* symphony, and the 30 s STARLIGHT loop = its sunny development; falling star and rainbow = Debussy, *Clair de lune*; the sun = the sunrise swell of *Morning Mood*; "all 24 and still hungry" = the cellos of Brahms 3 (3rd mvt). The **world** too: boardwalk = Mendelssohn Italian III, beach = Satie Gymnopedie 1 (guitar), sea = Smetana *Vltava*, hill = Brahms 3, summit = Mendelssohn *Scottish* Adagio, sky = Borodin *In the Steppes of Central Asia*. `tools/audio/real_music.py` cuts, gently levels (a slow leveller: 25 dB swings become ~10 dB), loops (equal-power cross-fade) and encodes them (OGG q5, 32 kHz, -23 dBFS RMS like before). The old synthesized pieces are kept in `tools/audio/old_music_r9/`. The credits name every composer and the recordings. |

## Notes
* I cannot listen: levels and loop seams were checked by measurement (loudness profiles, peak < 0.85, loop = equal-power blend of the end onto the start); please judge by ear. If one piece does not suit its zone, change the window in `real_music.py` (every piece is a function of start/end seconds) and run `python real_music.py <name>`.
* The raw recordings (about 1 GB of FLAC) are in `tools/audio/src/<name>/` and are not part of the repository.
* Sources (all marked "Public domain" on Wikimedia Commons): Grieg Morning Mood (Musopen Symphony), Dvorak Largo (Musopen), Chopin Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2 (Frank Levy), Mendelssohn Italian I and III and Scottish III (Musopen Symphony), Brahms 3 III (Musopen Symphony), Smetana Vltava and Borodin Steppes (Musopen Symphony), Debussy Clair de lune (Laurens Goedhart), Satie Gymnopedie 1 (Michael Laucke).
