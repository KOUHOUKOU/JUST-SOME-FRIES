# 32 · Round 11 — "subtractions and a calmer film" (2026-10-08: 6 points of the director's notes)

New dev mode: `--r10look` (the big brother from three angles, the task board, the see-through rainbow, Gull Sight with a rainbow fry). `--r10beats` now also checks that
the film moments never lock the gull and never freeze time.

| # | Note | What the game does now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The rainbow accessory blocks the view | The little rainbow over the head is **see-through** (alpha 0.3, no depth writing): it is still there, you look through it. |
| 2 | The right-hand "flight record" is noise | **Removed** (the panel, the per-flight statistics and everything that fed it). |
| 3 | The task list on the left is too unobtrusive | A real **task board**: a dark panel with a gold edge, a bigger heading (`EAT 6 FRIES  3 / 6`) with a progress bar, 14 px tasks, and the **first open task is the one to do now**: larger, pulsing, with one line under it that says where or how (`fish leap where the water bubbles`, `a cup or a cone in somebody's hand: take it`, ...). Done tasks stay ticked for 12 s. |
| 4 | The big brother's outfit is shabby | A wardrobe of his own (`gull_visual.gd _build_bro_item`, switched on by `bro_style`): a **crimson pinstripe coat** with gold-edged lapels, double buttons, gold epaulettes, a white **fur collar**, a pocket square and two long gold-hemmed tails; a tall **black satin top hat** with a gold band, a red jewel and a red-and-white **plume**; **gold-rimmed aviators**; a **double gold chain with a medallion**; the pipe stays. |
| 5 | The film moments are hasty and interrupt the flight | They are now **flow** moments (`story.gd flow_begin / inset_open / flow_end`, `story_scenes.gd`): **the gull keeps flying under the player's hands** (nothing is locked; no new grab or Gull Sight can start meanwhile), the world slows to 0.38 (0.2-0.26 for the last, thoughtful line), the picture **softens with a blur that grows towards the edges** (a screen shader), thin bars close in, and **other angles of the gull open as small tilted comic panels** (a second camera that flies along with the gull and looks for a free spot) with a word of their own (`COFFEE`, `LOOK UP`, `MAKE A WISH`, `THE WHOLE ARCH`, `BELOW`...). The comic words stay (`BZZZT! GLUG~ BRRR!`, `STARLIGHT`, `FWOOM!`, `TA-DAA!`, `24 / 24`). Lengths: 11-14 s (STARLIGHT 11, star 12.5, rainbow 12.6, sun and "all 24" 13); **the "still hungry" lines get 2.5-3 s each and the slowest time**. |
| 6 | The rainbow fry's TAB emblem is too big | Its emblem is **half the size** with a one-line, fainter label (no "NEED" line); the arrow at the screen edge is smaller. **The coloured pillar of light is unchanged.** |
| + | Found while playing through | **A memory leak in the comic lettering** (it existed since the opening of round 7): every frame of a word's pop-in used a new font size, and the text server bakes a new set of glyph textures for every size; the opening, the ending and the film moments cost 150-500 MB of video memory (the browser version would have been in danger). Words now use ONE size and the pop is a scale of the drawing: the opening goes from 319 MB / 229 MB video to 184 / 134, the 13 film moments from 676 / 468 to 207 / 156. The same was done to the pulsing "E" key of the rhythm. The little extra pictures (insets) reuse their viewports (a pool) and have no shadow atlas / MSAA. Also: the see-through treatment was given to the **cloud** accessory too (it hovers in the camera's way). |

## Notes
* The blur uses the screen texture with mip-maps (`filter_linear_mipmap`): please look at the web version once.
* A film moment never starts while the gull is locked on, carrying, or in a showcase; it waits like before. `GS.film_flow` blocks locks, Gull Sight and the codex while one runs.
