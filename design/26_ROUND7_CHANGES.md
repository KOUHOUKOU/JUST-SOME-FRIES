## The director's notes (point 10)
*What was wrong:* the opening was five lines of centred text and a speech bubble that hung over the big brother's head and filled the screen when the camera came close. The ending said "JUST WANT FRIES." in the middle of the screen. Nothing looked drawn, so nothing felt like a story.
*What I decided:* the 3D world is the artwork and the screen is **a comic page**. A paper margin and a thick ink border frame every shot, with halftone shading in two corners; the narrator speaks in yellow caption boxes; the characters speak in **real speech balloons** with a tail that points at the head (and stops above it, never on the face); sounds are lettered ("CLINK", "*puff*", "sniff sniff", "grrrmmbl"); a diagonal ink shutter wipes between panels. Every panel is a shot I composed on a contact sheet (`--cams`).
*The opening* is five establishing panels — the age of magic (an aerial of the town), the wind that listens to fries (a glowing fry beside the old man), breath sold by the carton (the Fry Shack's giant carton), a pier (the Ferris wheel), a seagull that is hungry (a stomach growl) — and then the big brother, who now has a body language: the chain clinks, the pipe puffs, he turns his head to ask. The little gull answers with a thought balloon: "...". It smells something warm (sniff sniff), the camera goes down to the cafe, and the last beat is yours: HOLD E.
*The ending* turns the joke into a title. The big brother asks again. Silence: the music drops away, the camera creeps in. **JUST** is slammed onto the page. Then everything the gull ever took falls off, one thing at a time in the order it was stolen. A small, humble **SOME**. Then **FRIES.** in gold. The three words form the name of the game: the answer to his question *is* the title. The picture cuts to the big brother, who takes his shades off ("...same."), the lettering still standing on the page. After the quiet cafe and the cards ("IT WAS ENOUGH."), the cover illustration fades in as the **poster** — the first picture of the game is the last one — and the credits roll over it.
*If you want to paint it:* every panel can be replaced by an illustration, see `docs/27_STORY_ART_PROMPTS.md`.

## Decisions that were not specified
* Hits no longer burn coffee / cocktail either (the stamina is the cost of a hit); ice cream and STARLIGHT still make the gull unhittable.
* STARLIGHT is triggered by picking up the third kind while the other two still run; it does not consume the drinks. It can be triggered again by a new cup once it is over.
* "Catch a fish" is a small task now (the quiet "still hungry" moment is kept for the fish book).
* The old man no longer puts the plain fry down in the opening: the plain fry stays hidden until the tutorial is over.
* The kind children only exist at the edges of the map (5 of 10 candidate spots were free of buildings).
* `--film` (the walkthrough recorder) was not updated to the new opening; re-record by hand or update `autotest.film`.
