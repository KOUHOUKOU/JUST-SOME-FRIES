jsf_sc.ttf and jsf_jp.ttf are small subsets (only the letters the game's Chinese and Japanese text uses, weight 500)
of Noto Sans SC and Noto Sans JP by Google, made by tools/i18n/build.py. They are licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (OFL.txt in this folder). "Noto" is a trademark of Google LLC; these subsets are renamed copies of the
fonts and are not endorsed by Google.
